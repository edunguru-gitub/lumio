## Running costs and break-even (slide 18)
- Running, a month: accountant 265 + AI and build tools 30 + server 35 + domains 11 = **341**. A year: 341 x 12 = **4,092**.
- Start-up, one time (estimate): **3,000** (range 2,000 to 5,000), team time not charged.
- Price (plan, excl. VAT): setup fee once 1,500 (range 800 to 2,500); subscription **1,200 per class a year** = 100 a month (range 900 to 1,800). Pilot: 300 commitment fee, not revenue, credited to the first invoice. Parents' premium: phase 2, hypothesis.
- **Cost to serve** one class a year = **228** = tax 12 + AI and voice 66 + support 150 (our time, valued).
- **Contribution per class** = 1,200 - 228 = **972**.
- Break-even: 4,092 / 972 = 4.2, so **5 classes**. With start-up: (4,092 + 3,000) / 972 = 7,092 / 972 = 7.3, so **8 classes**.
- Per school (base): 1,500 + 3 classes x 1,200 = 5,100; minus 3 x 228 (684), minus setup effort 600, minus 1% tax on the setup fee (15) = **3,801**. Break-even **2 schools**; 3 with start-up and acquisition.
- **Variable costs** (all costs that grow with customers, base) = **3,696** = cost to serve 6 x 228 (1,368) + setup effort 2 x 600 (1,200) + pilot AI 198 + free-learner AI 900 + 1% tax on setup fees 30.

## Why Lumio (slide 23)
- Tutor, maths, one year = 150 RON/h x 1 h/week x 35 weeks = **5,250** (Darius's own quoted rate; typical sourced range 75 to 150, top 200). Low 2,550 (75 x 34), high 7,200 (200 x 36).
- BAC year, three subjects: about **100 h** (maths 42, Romanian 29, third subject 29; all hours are our assumption) x 150 = **15,000** (estimate; low 7,500, high 20,000). New web sources were snippets, not opened.
- Lumio: 1,200 / 22 students = 54.5, **about 55** per student per year (price and class size are ours; 15 to 30 students gives 80 to 40).
- Ratios: 5,250 / 55 = 95x; 15,000 / 55 = 273x. Say "a fraction", not "273 times".
- Survey: families who pay spend **6,234** RON per child a year (Save the Children Romania, 683 parents, 2024-25, not representative, usually two subjects). About 1 in 3 pupils grades 5 to 12 take tutoring at about 300 RON a month (IRES/SAR, 2019-20, old).
- "Replacement for a tutor?" No: a tutor works 1:1; Lumio is practice between lessons. Prices only, no claim of equal results. Effect on grades: not found, not claimed.
