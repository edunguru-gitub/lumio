### A3. The 4 numbers I propose (all OPENED)
1. **6,234 RON** per child per school year, for Romanian families who pay for tutoring (Salvați Copiii, 683 parents, 2024-25 data).
2. **About 1 in 3** pupils in grades V-XII take tutoring, 300 RON a month on average (IRES/SAR, 2021; older, say "in 2021"); main reason: "the child does not understand the lesson at school".
3. **35% and 31%** of Romanian 15-year-olds report distraction by own / classmates' digital devices in maths lessons, above the OECD averages of 30% and 25% (PISA 2022).
4. **22%** of Romanian 11-15 year-olds show problematic social-media use, the highest of 44 countries (HBSC 2022).
Optional fifth for notes only: only 33% of Bucharest pupils say they always store the phone during lessons (about 4,000 pupils, not representative).

### A4. Slide-ready text, "Why us" (English, 74 words)
> **Why us**
> We are students. We know tutors are expensive: Romanian families who pay for tutoring spend about 6,234 RON per child per school year (Save the Children Romania, 2025). We know the phone steals attention: 35% of Romanian 15-year-olds report distraction by digital devices in maths lessons, 31% by classmates' devices (OECD PISA 2022). Banning phones teaches nothing. Lumio turns the phone from the enemy into a learning tool: lessons you play and touch.

Footer line (small): "Sources: Save the Children Romania (parent survey, n=683, 2024-25); OECD PISA 2022, Romania note." Notes for the speaker: "In our own survey, 10 of 13 students mostly read or watch to learn" (only this, per docs/pitch-stage2.md). Do not write "we pay for tutors" as a statistic; if it is true for the team it can be said as a spoken sentence, it is not data.
Caution: "Banning phones teaches nothing" is an opinion, not a finding; PISA actually says distraction is lower where phones are banned. The sentence is fair if kept as the team's view; if a juror pushes, answer "a ban removes the distraction, it does not give the student a way to learn; we want both".

**One-line pitch:** "Tutors cost families 6,234 RON a year and the phone costs students their focus; Lumio makes the phone the place where the lesson happens."

### B4. New business model inputs
- **One-time setup fee per private school** [A]: base **1,500 RON** (range 800 to 2,500), covers a teacher training session, class setup and onboarding support. Reasoning: our time cost is 12 hours x 50 RON = 600 RON [A]; 800 covers cost plus margin, 2,500 is the ceiling a small school may accept without a committee. It is 0.3% of one class's yearly tuition at the Iași private liceu (22,200 x 22 = 488,400) [D]. Benchmarks, all US vendors, SNIPPET: Labster 500 USD one-time fee (2,433 RON); Steegle year-one installation 2,500 to 6,000 USD (12,166 to 29,199 RON) per district incl. training; Sown to Grow implementation from 3,500 USD (17,033 RON); Renzulli PD day 1,200 USD online (5,840 RON). Our 800 to 2,500 is 7% to 21% of the cheapest US district fee: a deliberately small, first-customer price. A Romanian onboarding-fee benchmark: **not found**.
- **Subscription** [A]: 1,200 RON per class per year (range 900 to 1,800), shown as 100 RON per class per month, invoiced yearly up front.
- **Pilot commitment fee** 300 RON per pilot class: kept; credited against the first invoice if the school signs, so it is not counted twice. Pilot fees of schools that do not sign are ignored (conservative).
- **Parent premium** (100 RON hypothesis): moved to Phase 2, revenue 0 in year 1.
- Free outside learners (teacher links) and their AI cost stay as before (150 / 600 / 2,500 by Dec, 0.25 RON per learner-month, linear ramp): shown as acquisition cost.
- Support 150 RON per paying class valued, setup effort 600 RON per paying school valued [A].

### C5. Conflict of interest and GDPR (my view, not legal advice)
1. **Do mention it, briefly, in the Q&A or speaker notes, not on a slide.** Hiding a known link that organizers asked about is the larger risk; a jury that finds it later reads concealment, one that is told reads honesty. Suggested wording: "Full disclosure: our first contact school is one where our founder studied, and a family member of the team has worked with the school. We treat it like any other pilot: written agreement, same price list, and we do not count it as independent validation." Do not say "my father" or give names or the nature of the work; Manager should confirm with Eduard what exactly the work was before the sentence is used, and ask the organizers (via Teacher) whether such a link needs a formal declaration.
2. **Do not count Transylvania College as proof of demand.** Independent validation comes from schools without a link; present it as the first warm introduction only.
3. **Student-council friend:** do not name them; do not use a student (a minor?) as an official channel; the formal approach goes to the school's management or a teacher. No claim of interest from the school.
4. **GDPR:** any pilot involves minors. The school is the data controller; we need a short written data-processing agreement and consent handled by the school; Lumio should collect no names or personal data in the pilot, or only a class code (check what v1 stores: not verified here). The 'hint tutor' that sends text to a cloud AI must stay off in the pilot. Ask a lawyer or the organizers' GDPR guidance once (already item 5 in finance-pricing.md section 9).
5. Do not publish pupil results or photos from the school; use aggregated, anonymous numbers only.

### C6. Which tuition numbers support the price, and what a juror can challenge
| Tuition (per student per year) | Per class of 22 | 1,200 RON as share of class tuition | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transylvania College liceu, English line 62,000 lei | 1,364,000 | **0.09%** | SNIPPET, date unknown |
| ELF liceu 28,600 lei | 629,200 | 0.19% | SNIPPET |
| Iași private liceu 22,200 lei (15,600 low) | 488,400 (343,200) | 0.25% (0.35%) | SNIPPET (earlier file) |
| Brașov private 12,000 to 15,000 lei | 264,000 to 330,000 | 0.36% to 0.45% | SNIPPET |
| Avenor 8,000 to 12,000 EUR = 42,788 to 64,182 lei | 941,000 to 1,412,000 | 0.13% to 0.09% | SNIPPET |
Per student: 1,200 / 22 = 54.5 RON, which is 0.09% of 62,000 and 0.35% of 15,600 [D]. The earlier "about 0.3%" holds for Iași/Brașov-type schools; for Transylvania College it is 0.09%.
What a juror can challenge: (a) all tuition figures are press snippets with unknown dates, not school price lists; (b) private classes may be smaller than 22 (law: min 15), so at 15 pupils and 12,000 lei the share is 0.67% [D], still tiny; (c) tuition shows ability to pay, not willingness to pay for software: schools buy per teacher need, so adoption depends on the pilot; (d) a very low share can read as "too cheap, a toy" (Boyle's point), hence we keep 1,200 and add the setup fee; (e) a school licensing 3 classes pays 3,600 + 1,500, trivially small against a 1.3 million-lei class budget, which supports the price but also shows revenue per school is small: the model needs 2 to 3 schools, not one; (f) the funnel's 4% contact-to-customer rate has no source (B5 risk).
