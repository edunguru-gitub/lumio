### Typical mistakes (ASSUMPTION from teaching experience, to be checked with a maths teacher)
1. Substituting the infinity as a number: inf - inf = 0, or inf/inf = 1.
2. Thinking the limit equals f(a); forgetting that a limit does not need f defined at a.
3. Not checking one-sided limits (modulus, piecewise, 1/x).
4. Applying L'Hospital before checking the form (not in all programs).
5. Horizontal asymptote means the graph never crosses it (false).
6. Forgetting to compute both +inf and -inf asymptotes; mixing oblique asymptote slope formula m = lim f(x)/x.
7. "Limit exists" confused with "limit is finite".

### Interactive lab ideas (design suggestions, ASSUMPTION)
| Lab | What the student does | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Approach table | Pick x close to a from left/right, see f(x) values filling a table | builds the intuitive limit, shows one-sided |
| Graph zoom | Zoom to a hole/jump/asymptote, see behaviour | removable vs jump vs infinite |
| Epsilon-delta slider | Move epsilon, find delta | beyond BAC; optional "challenge" tier |
| Asymptote hunter | Drag x to infinity, tick horizontal/oblique line | maps to a BAC subiect III item |
| Indeterminate-form sorter | Classify forms, pick technique (factor, conjugate, divide by the highest power) | trains the typical exam computation |
Phone-as-controller fits the sliders (slider = epsilon or x).
