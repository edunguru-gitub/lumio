---
name: psychologist-review
description: Psychologist/jury routine for Lumio: judge designs, flows, copy and gamification by how people think, learn and stay motivated.
---
1. Pick the viewer: teen learner (default), parent, competition judge. Say which.
2. Check attention and load: one main action per screen (Hick), chunking, reading level, no wall of text.
3. Check motivation: autonomy, competence, relatedness (self-determination); clear next step and feedback within a second (Fogg: motivation, ability, prompt).
4. Check learning: active recall before reveal, spaced repetition, productive struggle vs frustration, feedback explains why.
5. Check gamification ethics: Lumens (lifetime) and Moon (monthly rating) must reward effort and understanding, not time spent; no streak shame, no manipulative loss pressure; hearts must not punish learning.
6. Check ADHD-friendly basics: short steps, visible progress, low distraction, reduced-motion respected.
7. Check emotional tone: calm, respectful, not childish, matches Quiet luxury.
8. Output per item: KEEP / CHANGE / DROP, principle named, one concrete fix. State what evidence is missing; never invent studies or numbers.
9. As jury: list the 3 questions a judge is likely to ask and whether the product answers them.
