### 1 Problem, client, validation (15)
- Customer is not always the consumer: "the customer is the school, the consumer is the student". Say who you sell to, why they buy (value proposition), how the end user gets the product. Daniel, ST 00:16:17 to 00:19:34.
- One-sentence template, "write it down": for [segment] with [pain], [product] is a [category] that gives [benefit]; "unlike consumer apps" it [differentiator] (competitors named). Daniel, ST 00:16:17 to 00:19:34 (his example was a speaking-practice app for teachers).
- Order of thinking: problem-solution fit, then product-market fit, then viability (why buy at this price), then ways to lower the barrier (freemium, school gives access, consumer financing); keep running feedback loops. Khan Academy was named as an example with three access routes (family, school, individual); the detail came from a stage I session not in the file. Daniel, QA 00:08:45 to 00:11:22.

Kaustav, QA 00:11:22 to 00:13:18. Changing the prototype after feedback is "a very important learning experience" (QA 00:31:22).
- Competitor analysis table: direct and indirect alternatives, including "do nothing / in-house / other agency"; compare product, service, strategy, quality, price; criteria are yours to change; answer "why should they move from what they use now". Daniel, QA 00:16:59 to 00:21:24.

## Jury checklist for Lumio (15 yes/no, tick before upload on 27 Nov)
1. Does the deck say in one line who PAYS (school, parent) and who USES (student), and that they differ?
2. Is there a one-sentence value proposition with "unlike [named competitors]"?
3. Does validation state only what really happened (13 survey answers, 5 testers) with sample size and limits, and nothing inflated?
4. Is every number traceable to the survey, the testers, the repo or a labelled assumption?
5. Does the competitor slide include indirect alternatives (what students do today) and one clear USP?
6. Is there one real step towards a payer (a school or teacher reply, pilot agreement, letter of intent), or an honest "none yet" plus a dated plan?
7. Are parental consent and GDPR for under-18 mentioned for interviews, pilot and video?
8. Does the tech slide separate what works today from what is planned?
9. Does it say honestly where AI is used (build process, read-aloud) and where it is not (handwriting recognition is not machine learning)?
10. Are all 9 Business Model Canvas boxes filled, consistent with the plan (students free, school licence, parent premium)?
11. Is there a reason why each payer pays, and how the free tier is paid for?
12. Are start-up and monthly costs real figures from Eduard, with a break-even formula and team time stated?
13. Is revenue shown as plain arithmetic (customers x price minus cost to serve) with no promised growth?
14. Do KPIs include return or retention, free-to-paid conversion, cost to reach a user, and one education-impact measure, each measurable in the pilot?
15. Does the pitch open with who/what/problem, end with a clear ask, let all three members speak with roles visible, stay under 5:00 and 25 slides?
